104-Key Full-Size Matrix Architecture

A full-size layout requires a grid of rows and columns (typically ~6 rows by 17 columns) to map all 104 switches.

Matrix Grid Strategy

For 104 switches, you will divide them into Rows and Columns. Ensure every single switch has a 1N4148 diode soldered across one pin with the black band pointing consistently toward the column or row bus.

Rotary Encoder Integration

Mounted neatly above the numpad or arrow cluster. Connect the 3 quadrature pins (A, GND, B) and the 2 push-button switch pins directly to available GPIOs on your Black Pill.

Hand-Wiring & Protection Steps

Detailed steps for assembling a robust full-size keyboard with dual RGB underglow strips.

Step 1

Diodes & Matrix Busbars

Solder an 1N4148 diode on every switch pin. Run solid core wire down columns and insulated wire across rows. Double-check for crossing short circuits.

Step 2

Rotary Encoder Hookup

Wire encoder A and B pins to GPIOs, center pin to GND, and switch push-button to a separate GPIO for volume and mute control.

Step 3

Dual WS2812B RGB Underglow Chaining

Chain 2 x 8-LED boards (16 LEDs total) along the base. Wire Board 1 DIN to Black Pill GPIO, DOUT to Board 2 DIN, and share 5V & GND rails.

Step 4

Electrical Protection Components

Add a 330Ω resistor on the RGB data line and a 1000µF capacitor across 5V/GND to protect against voltage spikes.
